LAB 3 · STARTER GAME

Coin Dash

Collect gold coins. Dodge magenta drones and the orange chaser. Grab a blue shield to be safe for a few seconds, or a violet magnet to pull coins toward player 1. How many points can you score in 45 seconds?

Move: arrow keys / WASD or drag on the game · Player 2: T, then IJKL · Pause: P / Esc · Restart: R or tap · Difficulty: 1 / 2 / 3 · Sound: on · M to toggle
